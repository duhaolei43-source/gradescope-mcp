# Agensi benchmark research

Reviewed 2026-10-17 through public marketplace pages, targeted search, education/productivity/workflow categories, and creator documentation. This is a comparative review of discoverable listings, not an exhaustive audit of every skill or a purchase/source-code test. "Quality" below means a useful observable product pattern: clear outcome, inspectable example, evidence handling, dependencies, and limitations. Ratings/install counts are platform-displayed signals, not independent proof.

## What the current platform actually says

[Seller page](https://www.agensi.io/sell) describes automated scanning, AI review and human review, and currently says 24–48 hours. Its earnings examples are explicitly illustrative; they are not verified creator sales. The page describes agent-native delivery as well as skill submission. Older listing pages still show ZIP download instructions. [Submission](https://www.agensi.io/dashboard/submit) exposed only a loading shell to the public fetch, so authenticated fields and upload limits were not verified. We prepare the root-SKILL.md ZIP requested by the owner, and keep the core instructions useful when loaded remotely.

[Format reference](https://www.agensi.io/learn/skill-md-format-reference) requires name and description frontmatter. [Packaging guidance](https://www.agensi.io/learn/turn-configs-into-sellable-skills) supports references, scripts and examples. The reported visitor count and specific student search demand in the outreach email were not independently substantiated.

The [browse page](https://www.agensi.io/skills) and indexed all-skills pages showed inconsistent catalogue totals; category filters sometimes returned no results despite visible category counts. Therefore no claim is made that we reviewed "all quality skills," that the education niche is empty, or that displayed counts establish market size.

No competitor skill was downloaded, executed or copied. Detail-page lookup for Daily Work Planner was unavailable; only its category description was assessed. Prices and engagement figures can change and are not a forecast.

## Critical competencies to ship

1. Trustworthy freshness: resolve IDs, paginate, verify scope and terminal coverage; unknown/missing data remain explicit. Runtime fixes prevent two observed false-complete paths.
2. Evidence-based feedback: applied versus unapplied rubric, normalized signs, comments/annotations, page citations, and clear OCR uncertainty.
3. Practical onboarding: local prerequisite doctor, bundled reviewed runtime/lockfile, predictable setup/recovery, and honest macOS/STDIO requirements.
4. Low-noise recurring value: deadline brief and change digest, with optional scheduling only on user request. Do not restart stopped automation.
5. Reviewable distribution: root metadata, licensed source, deterministic allowlist, per-file hashes, no personal data, reproducible synthetic proof.

Recommended launch: **Gradescope Study Companion — free macOS preview**. Differentiation is account-connected evidence and coverage-aware workflows, not a generic study prompt or an untested claim of universal agent support.
